description = Hold jump to fly. Multiplayer version of the built-in Jetpack: every player in the session needs the mod enabled. The host controls thrust, speed limit, fuel and who may fly from the pause menu (Jetpack Settings), and can choose whether other players see that menu at all. Pick a flight mode (Classic, Hover or Glide), use hover assist (flight + crouch), and earn upgrades through flight time. Includes a flight stats page, two custom profile slots, per-player thrust and endurance overrides, and an optional message to everyone when the host changes settings. Customise your HUD with gauge size, position and colourblind colours, a speed readout (m/s, km/h or mph) and an altitude readout. Works with keyboard and controller and has exhaust effects. Based on the built-in Jetpack by Tuxedo Labs. Please like on Steam if you like the mod! Thanks
